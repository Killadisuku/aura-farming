PRIVACY

Last updated: 31 August 2026

AURA is a social reputation app. Aura points have no cash value and cannot be purchased.

What we store

This web app currently stores your session and activity on your device (local storage). Demo data is preloaded on first visit. If you sign in with Google in a future release, we would receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google to create your account.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not charge money for Aura.
  • We do not run ads in the current version.
